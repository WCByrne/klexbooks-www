The Calendar tab is home base: everything you've booked, in a month grid or a day-by-day list.

![The month view, with jobs color-coded by type and a personal appointment shown in grey](/screens/iphone-calendar-month.webp)

## Month and list views

Use the toggle at the top to switch between the two.

- **Month** scrolls continuously through the weeks. Jobs are colored by job type. Tap a day to select it, then **+** to book a job on that day. Pinch to make the rows taller or shorter (on a Mac, **⌘+** and **⌘−**). When a day is too full to show everything, tap **+2 more** to see the rest.
- **List** shows your sessions day by day, starting from today. Today's jobs show the drive time and when to leave, with a button for directions, and finished sessions show the hours you logged.

![The list view, with today's job showing when to leave, the drive time and a Directions button](/screens/iphone-calendar-active-job.webp)

Tap **Today** to jump back to the current date from anywhere.

## Schedule around your personal calendars

Klex Books can show events from your other calendars (personal, family, a shared work calendar) so you don't book a job on top of the dentist.

1. Go to **Settings › Calendar**.
2. Under **Show as Busy**, allow calendar access if asked, then choose the calendars to include.

Those events appear greyed out on the Klex Books calendar and in the scheduler, and you'll get a warning if a session overlaps one. Tap a grey event to see what it is. To hide them for a moment, use the **Show Other Calendars** button in the Calendar toolbar.

Klex Books only reads these events to show them to you. It never changes them. The choice of calendars is made separately on each device.

## Show your jobs in the Calendar app

Going the other way, Klex Books can add your jobs to the Calendar app, so they show up alongside everything else, on your watch, and in any calendar you share with family.

1. Go to **Settings › Calendar**.
2. Turn on **Sync jobs to Calendar**.

Klex Books creates a calendar named "Klex Books" (you can rename it) and keeps an event there for every scheduled session.

A few things to know:

- **It's one-way.** Klex Books owns that calendar. If you move or delete one of its events in the Calendar app, Klex Books puts it back. Make schedule changes in Klex Books.
- **Don't point it at a calendar you already use.** If you connect to an existing calendar, Klex Books asks before replacing the events in it.
- **Turning it off** asks whether to delete the calendar or keep it. Your jobs aren't affected either way.
- **It's per device.** Turn it on from one device and the events will reach the others through your calendar account.
