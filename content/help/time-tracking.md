Hours you track turn straight into what you bill, so there's no timesheet to fill in at the end of the day.

![The Klex Books timer on the Lock Screen, with a Clock Out button and a one-hour-remaining reminder](/screens/iphone-live-activity.webp)

## Clock in and out

When you have a job today, a **Clock In** bar floats at the bottom of the app with the next job on your schedule. Tap **Clock In** to start the timer. If more than one job is scheduled today, you'll be asked which one, and **Other** lets you clock in on any job, scheduled or not.

You can also open any job and clock in from there.

While the clock is running, the bar shows the timer and a red **Clock Out** button. On iPhone and iPad the same timer appears on your Lock Screen as a Live Activity, so you can clock out without opening the app.

Only one clock runs at a time. To switch jobs, clock out of one and in to the next.

## Automatic clock-in

With **Settings › Time Tracking › Clock in automatically** turned on, Klex Books starts the clock for you at each session's scheduled start time. Forgot to open the app until an hour in? Your time still counts from the scheduled start.

- You stay clocked in until you clock out. Klex Books reminds you when the scheduled time is up, and before that when there's an hour left.
- If you delete an automatic entry, Klex Books takes that as "not this session" and won't create it again.
- If you're still on the clock for another job when a session starts, that session isn't started automatically.
- This setting is per device, so your Mac and your iPhone don't both clock you in. Turn it on for the device you carry.

Started late, or stayed longer than planned? Just edit the entry afterwards.

## Departure alerts

On iPhone and iPad, **Settings › Time Tracking › Departure alerts** puts a heads-up on your Lock Screen before each job, with the drive time from wherever you are and a **Clock In** button.

![A departure alert on the Lock Screen, showing when to leave and the drive time, with Directions and Clock In buttons](/screens/iphone-departure-alert.webp)

- The alert shows up early enough to cover the drive, plus 30 minutes.
- If the client has no address, you get a simple reminder an hour before the start instead.
- For drive times that stay accurate while the app is closed, allow location access **Always** when asked. With "While Using", the estimate only updates while Klex Books is open.

## Fix or add time by hand

Every job has a timesheet. Open the job, go to the **Items** tab and look under **Timesheet**.

- Tap **Add Entry** to log time you didn't clock, with a clock-in and clock-out time.
- Tap an entry to change its times, its hourly rate or its note, or to delete it.

Two small safety nets:

- **Clocked out by accident?** Clock back in on the same job within 60 seconds and your session picks up where it left off.
- **Clocked in by accident?** If you clock out within a minute, Klex Books asks whether to delete the entry.

## How time is billed

Each entry is billed at the job's hourly rate, rounded up to the next 15 minutes. A session of 1 hour 50 minutes bills as 2 hours.

Each entry remembers the rate it was created with. If you change a job's rate partway through, earlier entries keep the old rate unless you edit them.
