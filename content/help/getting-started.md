Klex Books is the back office for a one-person business: clients, schedule, hours, invoices, expenses and mileage in one app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. There's no account to create. Open the app and you're in.

![The Calendar tab in list view, showing today and the jobs coming up](/screens/iphone-calendar-list.webp)

## The two-minute setup

The first time you open Klex Books it asks a handful of questions. Every answer can be changed later in **Settings**, so don't overthink it.

1. **How do you charge for your work?** Pick **All jobs are the same** if every job uses one hourly rate and a typical length. Pick **Different kinds of work** if you do a few kinds of jobs at different rates (say, Handyman and Painting). Those become *job types*, each with its own rate, usual session length and calendar color.
2. **Time tracking.** Choose whether Klex Books should clock you in automatically when a scheduled session starts, and whether you want a departure alert before each job. More in [Tracking your time](/help/time-tracking).
3. **Travel.** Turn on automatic travel expenses, then add the address you start from and your mileage rate. Klex Books logs round-trip mileage for each day you work a job. More in [Expenses, receipts and mileage](/help/expenses-and-mileage).
4. **Contacts.** Optionally save your clients to the Contacts app so calls and texts show their name, and import people you already have as contacts.

## Finding your way around

Klex Books has five sections. On iPhone they're tabs along the bottom; on iPad and Mac they're in the sidebar.

- **Calendar** is your schedule, as a month grid or a day-by-day list.
- **Jobs** is everything you're working on, grouped into Today, Next 7 Days, Upcoming, Leads and Archive.
- **Clients** is your client list with contact details, job history and files.
- **Finances** shows income, expenses and profit by month or year.
- **Settings** holds your rates, business details, sync options and subscription.

On a Mac, press **⌘1** through **⌘4** to jump between the first four, and **⌘,** for Settings.

## Your first job, start to finish

1. In **Clients**, tap **+** and add a client. A first name is all that's required, but an address unlocks directions, drive-time alerts and mileage.
2. In **Calendar**, tap the day you want and then **+**. Choose the client, pick a job type if you use them, and tap **Add Session** to set the time.
3. On the day, tap **Clock In** when you start and **Clock Out** when you finish.
4. Open the job's **Billing** tab. Your hours are already totaled. Tap **Share Invoice** to send a PDF.
5. When the money arrives, tap **+ Payment** and record it.

That's the whole loop. The other articles go deeper on each step.

## Before you send your first invoice

Go to **Settings › Business & Invoices** and fill in your business name, email, phone and address. They appear at the top of every invoice. You can also set your payment terms and add payment instructions, like your Venmo handle or who to make checks out to.

## On more than one device

Klex Books syncs through your own iCloud. Install it on another device signed in to the same Apple Account and your data shows up on its own. See [iCloud sync, backup and export](/help/sync-and-export).
