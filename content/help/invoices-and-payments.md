Everything about a job's money lives on its **Billing** tab: the estimate, the invoice, payments and what you actually made.

![A job's Billing tab, showing the invoice total, a recorded payment and the profit](/screens/iphone-job-billing.webp)

## How a job is priced

What you bill is made of two things:

- **Labor**: the hours on the job's timesheet, at the job's hourly rate. See [Tracking your time](/help/time-tracking).
- **Line items**: anything else you charge for, like materials, a disposal fee or a fixed-price service.

Add those together and you have **Total to bill**.

### Charging a flat price

Not everything is hourly. To charge a fixed price, add a line item for it (say, "Gutter cleaning, $180"). If the whole job is flat-rate, set the job's hourly rate to $0 before you clock in. You can still track your hours to see how long the job took, and only the line items are billed.

## Add line items

1. Open the job and go to the **Items** tab.
2. Under **Line Items**, tap **Add Item**.
3. Enter a title, price and quantity.
4. Tap **Save**.

![A job's Items tab, with the timesheet, a line item and a mileage expense](/screens/iphone-job-items.webp)

If the item cost you something, turn on **Linked expense** and enter what you paid. The customer is billed your price, and your cost counts against the job's profit.

To pass a purchase straight through to the customer, record it as an expense and turn on **Bill to client**. See [Expenses, receipts and mileage](/help/expenses-and-mileage).

## Estimates

Before any work is done, the Billing tab shows an **Estimated total**: the scheduled hours at your rate, plus line items. Tap it to see the breakdown. It's a quick answer for "roughly what will this cost?"

## Send an invoice

1. Open the job's **Billing** tab.
2. Tap **Share Invoice**.
3. Send the PDF by Messages, Mail, AirDrop or anything else on the share sheet.

![An example invoice, with labor, items, a payment received and the balance due](/screens/invoice-example.webp)

The invoice lists labor and items, any payments already received and the balance due. Each job has its own invoice number, and it stays the same if you send the invoice again.

### What appears on your invoices

Set these once in **Settings › Business & Invoices**:

- **Business name, email, phone and address** appear at the top.
- **Payment terms** set the due date, counted in days from the job's last session. The default is 14 days.
- **Payment instructions** are printed at the bottom. Use them for your Venmo or Zelle details, or who to make checks out to.

## Record a payment

Klex Books doesn't process payments, so there are no fees. Get paid however you already do, then record it:

1. Open the job's **Billing** tab and tap **+ Payment**.
2. Enter the amount, date and method: Cash, Check, Card, Transfer, Venmo, Zelle or Other.
3. Tap **Save**.

![The Record Payment form, with amount, date, method and note](/screens/iphone-record-payment.webp)

Record a deposit or a part payment the same way. Klex Books keeps a running balance. Tap a payment to edit it, or swipe to delete it.

## Payment status

The badge next to **Invoice** tells you where a job stands:

- **Payment Due**: billed, not yet paid.
- **Partially Paid**: some money in, balance remaining.
- **Overdue**: the due date has passed.
- **Paid**: settled in full.

![A partially paid job, showing the payment received, the balance due and the due date](/screens/iphone-job-billing-partial.webp)

Everything you're still owed is added up under **Outstanding** in the [Finances](/help/finances) tab.

## What you actually made

At the bottom of the Billing tab, **Income** shows what you've been paid for the job, the job's expenses, and the profit left over.
