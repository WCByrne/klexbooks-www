Klex Books has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your data lives on your devices and syncs through your private iCloud, so we never see your books.

![Klex Books on a Mac, showing the same schedule as on iPhone](/screens/mac-calendar-list.webp)

## Use Klex Books on more than one device

1. Make sure each device is signed in to the same Apple Account, with iCloud turned on.
2. Install Klex Books on each one.

That's it. Your clients, jobs and everything else arrive on their own. On a new device with a lot of history, give it a few minutes on Wi-Fi.

## Check that sync is working

**Settings › iCloud Sync** shows your iCloud account status, whether everything is up to date, and when the last sync happened.

Changes normally sync within moments. If something looks behind:

- Tap **Sync Now** to send any changes waiting on this device.
- Check that the device is online and signed in to iCloud.
- Make sure iCloud is turned on for Klex Books in your device's iCloud settings.
- If the status says iCloud is temporarily unavailable, look for an Apple Account prompt in your device's Settings, or restart the device.

If you have more than one device, also check the other one. A change can't arrive until the device it was made on has sent it.

## Settings that are per device

Your data is the same everywhere, but a few switches are set separately on each device, because each device grants its own permissions:

- Clock in automatically
- Departure alerts
- Sync jobs to Calendar, and which calendars show as busy
- Sync clients to Contacts

So it's normal to have automatic clock-in on for your iPhone and off for your Mac.

## New phone?

Sign in to your Apple Account on the new phone, install Klex Books and open it. Your data syncs down from iCloud. You'll then want to turn the per-device settings above back on.

## Export your data

Your records are yours, and you can take a copy at any time, on any plan.

1. Go to **Settings › Export Data**.
2. Tap **Export…**
3. Save or share the zip file.

Inside you'll find:

- A spreadsheet file (CSV) for each kind of record: clients, jobs, sessions, time entries, line items, expenses, payments and more. They open in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.
- A `data.json` file with everything in full.
- Your photos and files.

It's a good thing to hand your accountant, and a sensible backup to keep somewhere safe once in a while.

## Stop syncing

To keep Klex Books on one device only, turn off iCloud for Klex Books in that device's iCloud settings.

## Privacy

There are no ads and no tracking in the app, and nothing is sold. Receipt reading and the written summaries happen on your device. The full details are in the [privacy policy](/privacy).
