The Finances tab answers the question "how's business?" without a spreadsheet.

![The Finances overview, with a written recap, income, expenses and net profit for the month](/screens/iphone-finances.webp)

## The overview

The big number at the top is your net profit for the period. Use the arrows to move between months, or tap the period name to switch between **Month** and **Year**.

Below it:

- **A recap in plain English.** A sentence or two on how the period went: up or down from the last one, how much you kept after expenses, and a highlight like your top client or best month.
- **Income, Expenses and Net.** The three numbers that matter.
- **Profit & Loss Statement.** Tap for the full breakdown: income by client and by job type, and expenses by category.
- **Last 12 Months.** A chart of income against expenses, so you can see the trend.
- **Outstanding.** What clients still owe you, with anything overdue called out. Tap **View Jobs** to see who.

## When money counts as income

Income is counted on the day you record a payment, not when you send the invoice. An unpaid invoice appears under **Outstanding** and moves into income once you mark it paid.

Expenses count on their own date.

## Income and Expenses tabs

- **Income** lists every payment you've recorded in the period, with a total.
- **Expenses** lists what you spent. Filter by category, search across all time, or tap **New** to add one.

Payments are recorded from a job's Billing tab. See [Invoices and payments](/help/invoices-and-payments).

## Insights (Pro)

With [Klex Books Pro](/help/free-and-pro), the overview adds:

- **Income by Job Type**: which kinds of work earn the most.
- **Top Clients**: who brings in the most money.
- **Next 30 Days**: what you're owed plus what's scheduled, so you can see what's coming.
- **Reconnect**: past clients worth a follow-up, with a message drafted for you.
- **Seasonal Trends**: your busy and slow months. This one appears once you have about six months of payments, and needs a device with Apple Intelligence.

![Pro insights in the Finances overview: top clients, the next 30 days and past clients to reconnect with](/screens/iphone-finances-insights.webp)

## Export for tax time

Pro can export the Profit & Loss statement for any month or year as a spreadsheet file (CSV) that opens in Numbers or Excel. Open **Profit & Loss Statement** and tap **Export**.

On any plan you can export all of your records from **Settings › Export Data**. See [iCloud sync, backup and export](/help/sync-and-export).

Klex Books keeps your records tidy, but it isn't tax software. Check with an accountant about what you can deduct.
