A job is one piece of work for one client. It holds the schedule, the hours you worked, what you're billing, what it cost you, and any photos or files.

![The Jobs tab, with jobs grouped under Today, Next 7 Days and Upcoming](/screens/iphone-job-list.webp)

## Create a job

1. Tap **+** in **Jobs**, or tap a day in **Calendar** and then **+**.
2. Under **Who**, choose the client. Recent clients appear as shortcuts; tap **All** to search everyone or add a new client.
3. Under **What**, pick a job type if you use them. The hourly rate fills in from the type (or from your default rate) and you can change it for this job.
4. Optionally add a title and notes. If you leave the title blank, the job is named after the client and job type.
5. Under **Schedule**, tap **Add Session** to set when it happens.
6. Tap **Save**.

![The New Job form, with recent clients under Who and job types under What](/screens/iphone-new-job.webp)

A job created from a calendar day starts with a session on that day at 9:00 AM. A job saved without any sessions becomes a **lead**: work you've talked about but haven't booked yet.

## Sessions

A *session* is one block of scheduled time. A quick job has one. A bigger job can have as many as you need, on any days: Tuesday morning, Thursday afternoon, and again next week.

New sessions start out at your usual length, which comes from the job type's **session duration** or from **Settings › Jobs › Job Defaults**. To add, change or remove sessions later, open the job, go to the **Details** tab and tap the schedule.

![A job's Details tab with two sessions on different days](/screens/iphone-job-schedule.webp)

## The scheduler

![The scheduler, warning that a session overlaps another job](/screens/iphone-scheduler.webp)

Adding or editing a session opens the scheduler. Set the date, start and end at the top, or use **Length** to make the session longer or shorter. Underneath is that day's timeline, showing your other jobs and any busy time from your personal calendars.

- **Drag the block** to move it to another time.
- **Drag its top or bottom edge** to change when it starts or ends.
- **Tap another day** in the week strip to move it there. The small bars under each day show how full that day already is.

Times snap to 15 minutes.

### Warnings

Klex Books flags three things as you schedule:

- The session **overlaps another job**.
- The session **overlaps a busy event** from one of your other calendars. ([Choose which calendars count](/help/calendar).)
- There **isn't enough drive time** between this session and the job before or after it, when the two are at different addresses.

These are heads-ups, not roadblocks. You can always tap **Done** and save anyway.

## Job status

You never set a status by hand. Klex Books works it out from the schedule and the clock:

- **Lead**: no sessions scheduled yet.
- **Upcoming**: scheduled, and the first session hasn't started.
- **In Progress**: the first session has started, or you've clocked in.
- **Completed**: the last session has ended.
- **Cancelled**: you cancelled it.

Finished and cancelled jobs move to **Archive** at the bottom of the Jobs list. Removing a job's only session turns it back into a lead.

## Cancel, reopen or delete

Open the job and tap **•••**:

- **Cancel Job** takes it off your schedule but keeps the record. Choose **Reopen Job** to bring it back.
- **Change Client** moves the job to a different client.
- **Delete** removes the job for good, along with its time entries, line items and expenses.

## Job types

Job types are for people who do more than one kind of work. Each has a name, an hourly rate, a default session duration and a calendar color, so a new "Painting" job arrives pre-priced and color-coded.

Manage them in **Settings › Jobs**. Tap **Add Job Type** to make your own or **Add from Presets…** to pick from common trades. Changing a type's rate only affects new jobs. Existing jobs keep the rate they were created with, and deleting a type leaves its jobs in place with their rates intact.
