![A job's Files tab, with buttons for Photos, Camera and File above two attached photos](/screens/iphone-job-files.webp)

## Add photos and files

1. Open a job or a client and go to the **Files** tab.
2. Tap **Photos** to choose from your library, **Camera** to take a picture on the spot (iPhone and iPad), or **File** to pick a PDF or image file.

Tap any file to open it. To remove one, touch and hold it (or right-click on a Mac) and choose **Delete**.

## Job files and client files

- Files added on a **job** stay with that job.
- A **client's** Files tab shows everything attached to the client directly, plus the files from all of their jobs, newest first. It's the quickest way to find "that photo from last spring".

Receipts are kept separately, with the expense they belong to. See [Expenses, receipts and mileage](/help/expenses-and-mileage).

## Share from Photos and other apps

You don't have to open Klex Books first.

1. In Photos, Files or another app, select what you want and tap **Share**.
2. Choose **Klex Books**. (The first time, you may need to tap **More** to find it.)
3. Pick the job or client to add it to.
4. Tap **Save**.

If Klex Books says it isn't available, open the app once and try again.

## Limits on the free plan

The free plan includes up to 10 files per job. Files on clients aren't limited. [Klex Books Pro](/help/free-and-pro) removes the limit, and nothing you've already attached is ever removed if you go back to free.

## Files and iCloud

Files sync through iCloud like everything else, but they're bigger, so on a new device they can take a little while to arrive. If a file says it hasn't finished downloading, give it a moment on Wi-Fi and try again.
