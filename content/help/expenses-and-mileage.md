Tracking expenses as you go means you know what each job really earned, and tax time is a lot less painful.

## Add an expense

There are two places to start:

- **For a job:** open the job, go to the **Items** tab and tap **Add Expense**.
- **For the business in general:** go to **Finances › Expenses** and tap **New**.

Enter the amount and choose a category: **Travel/Gas**, **Training/Certifications**, **Supplies** or **Other**. Title, vendor, date and notes are optional. You can link an expense to a job at any time using the **Job** field.

![The New Expense form, with Scan Receipt at the top](/screens/iphone-new-expense.webp)

## Scan a receipt

In the expense form, tap **Scan Receipt**.

- **On iPhone or iPad**, point the camera at the receipt, or choose a photo or PDF you already have.
- **On a Mac**, choose a file, or pick **Scan with iPhone or iPad** to use a nearby device's camera.

Klex Books reads the receipt and fills in the expense for you, and keeps the scan attached under **Files**. On devices with Apple Intelligence it fills in the vendor, a short title, the total, the category and a note of what was bought. On other devices it picks out the total and you fill in the rest. Everything is read on your device, and it's always worth a glance at the amount before you save.

![An expense filled in from a scanned receipt, with the vendor, amount, category and notes](/screens/iphone-expense-scanned.webp)

You can also attach photos or files to any expense by hand.

## Bill an expense to the client

Bought materials for a job? Pass the cost on:

1. Open the expense and make sure it's linked to the job.
2. Turn on **Bill to client**.
3. Leave **Billed amount** alone to charge what you paid, or enter a different amount to add a markup.

The expense is added to the job's invoice, and it still counts as your cost when Klex Books works out the job's profit.

## Automatic mileage

Klex Books can log your driving for every job without you touching a thing.

### Set it up

1. Go to **Settings › Travel & Mileage**.
2. Turn on **Add travel expenses automatically**.
3. Enter your **home address** (wherever you usually start from).
4. Enter your **mileage rate**. In the US this is usually the IRS standard mileage rate, which changes each January, so update it at the start of the year.

### How it works

- The first time you clock in on a job each day (or add a time entry for that day), Klex Books adds a **Travel** expense to the job for the round trip between your home address and the client's address.
- The distance is the driving distance, worked out once per client and remembered.
- You get one trip per job per day, however many times you clock in and out.
- After that it's an ordinary expense. Edit the miles or amount, or delete it, and Klex Books won't change it back.

![A job's Items tab, with a round-trip mileage expense added automatically under Expenses](/screens/iphone-job-items.webp)

### If mileage isn't being added

Open the job's **Items** tab. If a trip couldn't be added, a note under **Expenses** says why. The usual causes:

- The client has no address.
- Your home address or mileage rate isn't set in Settings.
- The address couldn't be found, or there's no driving route to it. Check for typos.

If the calculated distance isn't right for a client (you always leave from somewhere else, say), open the client, tap **Edit** and enter the **Round-trip miles** yourself.

## Review your spending

![The Expenses tab in Finances, with the month's expenses and total](/screens/iphone-finances-expenses.webp)

**Finances › Expenses** lists everything for the month or year you're viewing, with a total. Use the filter to narrow it to one category, or the search field to find any expense you've ever recorded.
