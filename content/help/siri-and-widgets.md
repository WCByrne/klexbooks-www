## Ask Siri

Say "Klex Books" as part of the request so Siri knows where to look. Some things to try:

- "Do I have any jobs today in Klex Books?"
- "What jobs do I have this week in Klex Books?"
- "What's coming up in Klex Books?"
- "Add a client to Klex Books"
- "Add a job to Klex Books"
- "Schedule a session in Klex Books"

Siri asks for anything it needs, like the client's name or the day and time. When you add a job, it asks whether to schedule it now or leave it as a lead. Tap a job or client in Siri's answer to open it in the app.

## Shortcuts

The same actions are available in the Shortcuts app, so you can put them on your Home Screen, the Action button, or into your own automations:

- **Create Client**
- **Add Job**
- **Add Session to Job**
- **Today's Jobs**, **This Week's Jobs** and **Upcoming Jobs**
- **Open Job** and **Open Client**

## The Upcoming Jobs widget

The widget shows the job you're on or the next one up, and what's coming after it. It comes in small, medium and large.

![The Upcoming Jobs widget on a Home Screen, showing a job starting soon and the days ahead](/screens/iphone-widget.webp)

To add it on iPhone or iPad:

1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen.
2. Tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**.
3. Search for **Klex Books**, choose a size and tap **Add Widget**.

Tap a job in the widget to open it in Klex Books.

## On the Lock Screen

Departure alerts and the running timer appear on your Lock Screen on their own, with buttons to clock in and out. Those are covered in [Tracking your time](/help/time-tracking).
