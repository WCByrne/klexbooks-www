Klex Books is free to use for as long as you like. Pro is there when your business outgrows the free plan's limits.

## What's free

- Up to **5 active jobs** at a time
- Up to **10 photos and files** per job
- Scheduling, the time clock and departure alerts
- Invoices, payments, expenses and mileage
- The Finances overview
- iCloud sync and data export

There's no trial clock and no card needed.

### What counts as an active job?

Any job that isn't finished or cancelled: leads, upcoming jobs and jobs in progress. Once a job's last session has ended it stops counting, which frees up a spot. You don't need to mark anything as done.

If you're at the limit and need room, cancel or delete old leads you won't be doing.

## What Pro adds

- **Unlimited active jobs**
- **Unlimited photos and files** on every job
- **Insights**: income by job type, top clients, seasonal trends and what's coming in over the next 30 days
- **Reconnect**: past clients worth following up with, and a drafted message
- **Profit & Loss export** for tax time

![Seasonal Trends, a Pro insight showing which kinds of work peak in which seasons](/screens/iphone-seasonal-trends.webp)

Pro is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, and your first month is free. Prices are in US dollars and may differ in other countries.

## Upgrade

Go to **Settings › Klex Books Pro** and tap **Upgrade to Pro**. You'll also be offered Pro whenever you reach a free limit.

Your subscription is tied to your Apple Account, so Pro works on your iPhone, iPad and Mac with one purchase.

## Manage or cancel

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To change plans or cancel, go to **Settings › Klex Books Pro › Manage Subscription**, or open your Apple Account's subscriptions in the App Store.

To avoid being charged, cancel at least a day before the free month or the current period ends. You keep Pro until then.

## If you stop paying

Nothing is hidden and nothing is deleted. Every job, client, file and report stays exactly where it is. You just can't add new jobs past the free limit, or more than 10 files to a job, until you're back under.

## Restore purchases

If you've subscribed but the app shows the Free plan (after setting up a new device, for example), go to **Settings › Klex Books Pro** and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
