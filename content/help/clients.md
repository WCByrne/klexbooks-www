Every job belongs to a client, so this is where things start. A client's page keeps their contact details, job history, billing and files together.

![The Clients tab, with each client's last job and a badge for upcoming work or leads](/screens/iphone-client-list.webp)

## Add a client

1. Go to **Clients** and tap **+**.
2. Enter a first name. Everything else is optional: last name, nickname, phone, email, address, notes and a photo.
3. Tap **Save**.

A couple of fields are worth knowing about:

- **Nickname** replaces the full name everywhere in the app. Handy for "Mrs. P" or "The lake house".
- **Address** powers the Directions button, drive-time warnings when scheduling, departure alerts and automatic mileage. If a client has no address, those features skip them.
- **Round-trip miles** is filled in automatically from the address once travel expenses are turned on. Type a number to override it, or leave it blank to let Klex Books calculate it.

You can also add a client on the fly while creating a job: in the New Job form, tap **All** (or **New**) under **Who**, then **New Client**.

## Import clients from Contacts

If your clients are already in your phone, there's no need to retype them.

1. Go to **Settings › Contacts**.
2. Tap **Import Clients from Contacts…**
3. Select the people you want and tap **Import**.

Anyone who's already a client is marked so you don't add them twice.

## Keep Contacts in sync

Turn on **Settings › Contacts › Sync clients to Contacts** and Klex Books saves each client as a contact card, in a list called "Klex Books". The payoff: when a client calls or texts, your phone shows their name and photo.

- You can rename the list from the same screen.
- To leave one client out, open the client, tap **Edit** and turn off **Save to Contacts**.
- This setting is per device, because each device grants its own access to Contacts.

## The client page

![A client's page on the Contact tab, with buttons to message, call, email and get directions](/screens/iphone-client-contact.webp)

Open a client to see four tabs:

- **Contact** has their details and notes, with quick buttons to message, call, email or get directions.
- **Jobs** is their job history. Tap **New** to start a job for them.
- **Billing** shows the totals: number of jobs, lifetime revenue and when you last worked together.
- **Files** holds photos and PDFs attached to the client, plus everything attached to their jobs. See [Photos and files](/help/photos-and-files).

## Slow day? Leads and Reconnect

When nothing is booked today, the Calendar list and Jobs list show a **No jobs today** card with two shortcuts:

- **Review Leads** lists jobs you've created but haven't scheduled yet.
- **Reconnect** lists past clients with nothing scheduled in 90 days or more, with your best-paying clients first. From the **Contact** menu you can message, call or email them, or choose **Draft Follow-Up…** to start from a friendly check-in note that you can edit before sending. Reconnect is a [Pro feature](/help/free-and-pro).

If there's a client you'd rather not be nudged about, open their page and turn on **Don't suggest reconnecting** (shown with Pro).

## Delete a client

Open the client, tap **Edit**, then **Delete Client**. Their jobs are kept, but will no longer have a client attached.
